# Project Handover

- Updated: 2026-09-29
- Version: 0.1.2
- Shared branch: `main`

## Current state

The project is in concept definition. `docs/CONCEPT_OVERVIEW.md` is the primary
explanation of the idea and the starting point for the next discussion. It
defines the problem, possible product, users, benefits, evidence model,
limitations, and open product decisions in plain language.

Existing code, dashboard files, model descriptions, benchmarks, and research
documents are exploratory material. They do not define the final product or
authorize further prototype work. Some claims in older documents still require
sources, real-data evaluation, and user validation.

## Immediate next work

1. Review `docs/CONCEPT_OVERVIEW.md` with the project owner.
2. Select the first user and the single decision Ghost Fleet should improve.
3. Choose the initial geography, vessel/cargo scope, evidence standard, data
   freshness, and project objective.
4. Turn those choices into an approved product brief before planning or building
   another prototype.

## Known risks and decisions

- No primary customer or first decision has been selected.
- Previous documents sometimes describe prototype features as finished product
  capabilities. Treat them as hypotheses until validated.
- Current cargo, risk, accuracy, latency, pricing, and market-value claims are
  estimates or research assumptions and need documented evidence.
- AIS gaps, vessel encounters, draft readings, radar matches, and risk scores do
  not independently prove wrongdoing.
- Data availability, freshness, and commercial licensing will constrain the
  eventual product.
- Do not continue implementation until the project owner gives product direction.
